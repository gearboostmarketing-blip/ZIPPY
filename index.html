How it works

1. Import the log, paste lines, or sync phones. Everything lands in the right month by its date, and nothing is ever cleared. 2. Pick a month at the top of Log, Books or Receipts. 3. In the Log, fix anything highlighted yellow and set each line's type and currency. 4. Books shows the month in US dollars, carries the balance on from last month, and keeps the month's safe counts. 5. Receipts builds one official receipt per expense; export the month's report and receipts as PDF or Excel.
